# Problems

Everything unresolved. Grouped by module, deep modules first.

Kinds: **defect** (fix it, quick) · **decision** (a person settles it) · **measurement**
(one look at data answers it) · **exploration** (a measured issue needing a loop).

**Only explorations get looped**, and only after being measured. See `CLAUDE.md`.

An entry carries, beyond its kind: the **seam** it happens at (`<engine>/SYSTEM.md`), an
**example**, how it was **measured** and at what n, the **hypothesis** or "none yet", and
**what has been ruled out** — each elimination with its measurement. The ruled-out part is
what licenses moving an issue out of the baseline and into exploration; see `METHOD.md`.

Seeded 2026-09-03 from the design discussion.
