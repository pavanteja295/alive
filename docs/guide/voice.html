Stage 2 · engines/text2audio

Their voice

The answer is spoken by F5-TTS, an open text-to-speech model that copies a voice from a ten-second sample. Out of the box it already sounds like them. This stage cleans four or so hours of their speech, trains F5 further on it (a finetune) so it also picks up how they pace and pitch a sentence, and picks the sample it copies from. About an hour and a half on one card.

How it speaks

Why the clip matters: one measured case

For Dr K the first clip was picked because it sounded the most like him to a speaker-matching model. It was also higher than his usual pitch, and every sentence the voice spoke came out higher too. The speaker-matching score did not move, because that model is built to ignore pitch.

How it is built

Why each cleaning step exists

stepwhat would go wrong without it
held-back videosScored on sentences from a video it trained on, a voice looks better than it is. Whole videos are held back, the same ones the face is scored on, so no stage learns from what another is tested on.
own transcript, not YouTube'sYouTube captions are tidied. A model trained on words that differ from the audio learns the difference.
cut on pausesCutting every 10 s slices words in half and teaches the model to start mid-breath.
speaker filterOne clip of a guest or a quoted voice shifts the voice the model settles on, and nothing measures it later. On their own channel the most common voice is them, so each clip is compared to that.
one loudness per videoVideos recorded months apart differ by ~10 dB. Levelling each whole video removes that; levelling each clip would also flatten how they get louder when they mean it.

How a saved model is judged

Three measurements per saved step, each read against what the creator's own real held-back speech scores, because even real speech does not score perfectly.

measurequestionwhat it can and cannot tell
similaritydoes it sound like them?Always about 99% of their own score, even before training: F5 copies the sound of a voice from the clip alone. Confirms the voice is them; cannot choose a step.
word errorscan Whisper read back the words?Catches slurring. Huberman: 0.4% for the voice, 1.3% for his own real speech.
pace and pitchsentence length, pitch range, pauses vs. the real sentenceThe only one that sees what training adds (14_prosody.py).

Commands

./alive build <creator> --stage voice runs the lot, stopping at the decisions. By hand, from engines/text2audio/blocks/voice/recipes/text-to-voice/:

python3 tools/status.py --profile <name>      # what is done, and the next command to run

Run what it prints, then ask again. A new creator starts as a copy of profiles/_template.py with three things filled in: the creator's name, their takes folder, and which videos to hold back. The full recipe, with every number from both creators, is RECIPE.md in that folder.
