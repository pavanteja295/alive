Stage 5 · engines/audio2face/gauss, built on STAvatar

Face motion to picture

Stage 4 gives a moving 3D face with no skin, hair or light. This stage learns to paint it as the creator, from their own video frames, so that whatever the mesh does, the picture looks like them doing it.

How a face is drawn: blobs stuck to a mesh

The renderer is STAvatar (a published method; credits). It draws the head as a cloud of about 80,000 tiny soft coloured blobs (3D Gaussians: each a fuzzy ellipsoid with a colour and a transparency). Each blob is attached to a triangle of the face mesh, so when the mesh moves, the blobs ride along. A small network adds a nudge per blob for each expression: the crease that appears in a smile, the inside of the mouth.

How it learns

What was changed from STAvatar as published

changewhy
the mesh is the MetaHuman rig, with teeth and eyeballs added, not FLAMEso stage 4's output plugs straight in; the teeth make an open mouth read as a mouth
many short clips merged into one training setSTAvatar expects one long, steady video; Dr K's training set is 162 chunks
only frames where they face the cameraturned heads smeared, and the live app shows them facing forward
blink amount measured from the real eyesthe sound cannot say when someone blinks

Our changes are kept as one patch (env/patches/stavatar.patch) applied on a fixed STAvatar commit by env/setup_externals.sh.

Putting the face on their real body

A floating head looks wrong. So the live app pastes the drawn head into real footage of the creator, and only the head is new: shoulders, hands and room are the original video.

Commands

./alive build <creator> --stage render runs it. By hand, from engines/audio2face/:

~/miniconda3/envs/stavatar/bin/python gauss/recipes/mesh-to-render/tools/status.py --profile <name>

# render a held-back clip, on its real body, with sound:
~/miniconda3/envs/stavatar/bin/python gauss/recipes/mesh-to-render/tools/infer.py --profile <name> --run <run> \
    --clip <chunk> --camera clip --pose replay:<chunk> --composite

The profile has the same name as the creator's motion profile and reads most of what it needs from it. The full recipe is engines/audio2face/gauss/recipes/mesh-to-render/RECIPE.md.
